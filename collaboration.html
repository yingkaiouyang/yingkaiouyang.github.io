Collaborations

Realising Quantum Error Correction

I am actively seeking new collaborative opportunities to advance the frontiers of quantum computing, particularly in the topics of quantum metrology and quantum error correction (QEC).

Permutation-Invariant Codes are awesome quantum error correction codes

Implementing reliable quantum error correction is a formidable challenge, making the selection of optimal codes for specific applications critically important. My quantum error correction research is often on permutation-invariant codes, which apart from their mathematically pleasing property of affording permutational symmetry, also offers different, and potentially advantageous pathways to implementation in actual quantum hardware.

Let's Work Together
