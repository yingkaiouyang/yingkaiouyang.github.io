Tight Cramer-Rao Bound for multiparameter quantum estimation theory

We introduce a conic programming framework for bounds in multiparameter quantum estimation theory, establishing a hierachy of bounds from a family of conic programs. The objective function of the conic programs are the same. Only the constraints differ. The cones we consider obey a nested structure thereby giving rise to this hierachy of bounds. The ultimate tight Cramer-Rao bound is the one for non-collective measurement strategies, and we give tight upper and lower bounds for it.
